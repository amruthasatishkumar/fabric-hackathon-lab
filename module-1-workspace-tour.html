Data Estate & Solution Architecture

This module introduces the Sudsberry property assessment scenario, the three source systems, the shared business keys that tie them together, and the medallion you'll build. Use it as a map for the goal modules that follow.

Scenario - The Municipality of Sudsberry

Sudsberry is a fictional municipality that assesses property values, tracks sales and inspections, and processes assessment appeals. Its data lives in three very different systems, and the assessor's office wants one governed view for analytics and natural-language questions.

🏠 VALUATION & SALES

Azure SQL - system of record

Relational: Jurisdiction, Neighborhood, PropertyClass, Parcel, Building, Assessment, Sale, and TaxRate tables, plus a vw_PropertyTaxRoll view exposing combined rate and estimated tax.

📋 PROPERTY PROFILES

Cosmos DB - property profiles

Documents: one JSON profile per parcel (partitioned by /neighborhoodId, id = parcelId) with nested jurisdiction, site, building, current assessment, and an inspections[] array.

📝 APPEALS

ADLS Gen2 - appeal narratives

Files: assessment appeals as equivalent JSON and Parquet, including a free-text narrative for AI enrichment and a groundTruthSentiment label for evaluation.

🔑 SHARED KEYS

How the sources join

parcelId joins parcels, assessments, sales, profiles, inspections, and appeals. neighborhoodId, taxYear, and propertyClassCode group and align the data. Normalize key casing and types in Silver before joining.

💡
Why one platform. Three sources, three shapes (relational, document, file) - and one Fabric workspace that unifies them on OneLake without copying data out of the sources. The assessor's office can then ask questions in plain English and get answers grounded across valuations, sales, inspections, and appeals.
⚠
Synthetic data. All addresses, parcels, assessments, sales, and tax figures are fabricated. Map coordinates are demonstration points. AI outputs are decision support only and must be reviewed before any use.

Recommended Gold marts

  • property_assessment_mart - parcel, class, neighbourhood, building, latest assessment, estimated tax, latest inspection.
  • comparable_sales_mart - arm's-length sales matched to the closest assessment year, with assessment-to-sale ratios.
  • appeal_intelligence_mart - appeal lifecycle plus AI sentiment, summary, classification, and follow-up signal.
  • neighborhood_equity_mart - valuation, sales-ratio, appeal-rate, and sentiment aggregates by neighbourhood.
