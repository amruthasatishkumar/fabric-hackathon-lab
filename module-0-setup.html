Welcome to the Fabric Workshop

Over the next few hours you'll work through a single, real-world use case end to end inside Microsoft Fabric, from raw sources all the way to an AI-grounded data agent.

We've prepared the use case for you to explore, the environment is already provisioned and waiting for your sign-in, and our team is here to support you at every step. Everything runs in the browser, so there's no Cloud PC, VS Code, or local install to set up.

Setup Checklist

Complete these checks, then continue with the lab.

  1. Log in and review your account

    Open a browser in incognito or guest mode to avoid overlap with any other Azure accounts you're signed into, then sign in with your assigned lab account (see Login Account above).

    If you're prompted to update your password, change it to User_Pwd#ESRi^Fbr!Hack<N>, where <N> is your assigned user number.

    If you're asked to set up Authenticator, install the Microsoft Authenticator app on your mobile phone and use it to complete the setup.

  2. Sign in to the Azure portal and verify shared RG access

    Go to portal.azure.com and open your resource group rg-fabric-user<N>-use2-dev, where <N> is your assigned user number.

  3. Sign in to Fabric

    Go to app.fabric.microsoft.com, open Workspaces, and confirm your workshop workspace is visible.
